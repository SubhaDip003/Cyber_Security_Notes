SecureCorp recently launched an AI fleet platform that allows users to control multiple AI agents from a single interface. Due to a misconfiguration, the agents" skill files were accidentally exposed to users.

---

### Description

The platform relies on skill files to define the capabilities, instructions, and behavior of its AI agents. As a security tester, your objective is to identify the exposed skill files, analyze the information they contain, and determine how it can be used to interact with the agents in an unintended way. Use your findings to exploit the exposed functionality and retrieve the flag.

**Objective**

Exploit the exposed AI agent skill files to retrieve the flag.
